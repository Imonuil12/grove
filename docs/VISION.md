# Vision

## The agenda

Grove helps people build the habits behind a healthy, capable life: physical, cognitive and personal
development. It does that with methods that research shows work, and it shows users the research.

## Who it's for

People who want to improve themselves in more than one area at once (read more, train, sleep better,
think more clearly) and who have tried apps that rely on willpower and guilt.

## The product

Three trackers built on one loop, and two features that cover all three areas:

```
          plan  →  do  →  log  →  reflect  →  share
Habits    "After I pour coffee, I read 10 pages"     (any recurring action)
Books     add a book, reading sessions, notes, recall questions
Training  strength, runs and cardio, body metrics

Coach     knows your logs; weekly review and one adjustment per week
Feed      milestones worth celebrating, shared with followers
```

Navigation: **Today · Habits · Books · Training · Coach · Feed · Profile**

## Principles

1. **Evidence or honesty.** Every mechanic is backed by research in [research/EVIDENCE.md](research/EVIDENCE.md),
   or it is labeled as a product choice. We never overstate a finding.
2. **Small and specific beats big and vague.** Plans name a cue, a time and a place; habits start tiny.
3. **Consistency over perfection.** A missed day is normal. The rule is "never miss twice," not a streak that resets to zero.
4. **Support, not guilt.** Copy and coaching support the user's autonomy and sense of progress (self-determination theory).
5. **Measure what matters, gently.** Trends over single data points (for example a 7-day weight average).
6. **Your data is yours.** Encryption at rest, private by default, sharing is opt-in.

## What Grove is not

- Not medical advice and not a diagnosis tool. Nutrition and training guidance stays within public health guidelines.
- Not an infinite-scroll social network. The feed exists for accountability and celebration.
