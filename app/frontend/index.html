About Grove

Grove is a place to grow every part of yourself: how you think, how you move, what you make, and who you are becoming.

Why it exists

Most self-improvement tools are either content you read and forget, or checklists with no understanding behind them. Grove joins the two: learn why something works, practice it in small daily steps, and measure the change over time.

How it works

A short check-in finds what you already know. An adaptive path fills the gaps, an AI coach adjusts to how you like to learn, and your tree grows a branch for every area you invest in.

Grove is for education and self-development. It does not replace advice from a doctor, coach or other qualified professional.
